How we work

Five clear steps, with a review at the end of each one.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We visit your space, listen to your needs and agree a budget and timeline.

  2. 2

    Concept

    You receive a moodboard, layout options and a palette of materials.

  3. 3

    Design

    We prepare detailed drawings and realistic 3D views for your approval.

  4. 4

    Build

  5. 5

    Handover

    We style the space, check every detail and hand you the keys.
