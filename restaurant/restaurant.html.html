👩‍🍳Our kitchen

Home cooking, by the sea

Spice Coast Kitchen started as a family dining room in Kovalam. We still cook the way we learned at home: fish bought each morning, spices ground in small batches and curries given time to settle.

Our menu follows the Kerala coast, from Malabar biryani in the north to Travancore fish curries in the south. Come for a quiet lunch, a family dinner or a table by the window at sunset.
